# Bday-letter
App pour anniversaire et astrologie
L'Histoire de ton Jour — Harry 🌅A beautiful, interactive birthday book app designed as a personal gift for Harry. Built with a rich twilight-sunset design and elegant typography, it tells the story of Harry’s birth date—September 17, 2004—across various cultural, historical, and cosmic lenses.
✨ Features
Interactive Navigation: Smooth page-by-page slideshow transitions optimized for both desktop and mobile screens.
Astrological Profile: Insights into Harry's Virgo sun sign, Libra moon, and ruling celestial bodies.
Historical & Cultural Roots: Features global traditions connected to September 17, including Christian, Islamic (Hijri), and Indigenous Moon of the Harvest calendars.
Retro 2004 Vibe: Relive the weather, pop-culture, hit music tracks (Ciara, Maroon 5), and tech trends from the day he was born in Montreal.
Life Milestones & Counter: A live calculated perspective of metrics like days lived, heartbeats, and upcoming "secret life milestones."
Time Capsule: A warm decade-by-decade projection looking forward into future milestones up to the year 2071.
Photo Placeholders: Custom sections ready to fit personal Polaroid-style family images.
Print to PDF: Built-in engine to easily convert the interactive web presentation into a perfectly formatted, printable A4-portrait book structure.
🛠️ TechnologyPure HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript (No heavy dependencies, loads instantly anywhere).Styled with tailored font pairs imported from Google Fonts (Playfair Display & Inter).
